# Internal AI-use policy

Policy version: `AI-POL-2026-08-15`

## Purpose

Quillhaven Academy uses AI only when the responsible owner can explain the use, preserve evidence, and route consequential decisions to an authorized human.

## Learner and public transparency

- A person directly interacting with an AI system must receive a clear notice before or at the first interaction unless the AI nature is obvious from the context.
- Public synthetic images, audio, or video must retain machine-readable provenance when the tool supports it. The Communications owner also requires a visible label unless Legal approves a documented exception.
- Staff-only drafting tools are not automatically treated as public interactions. The owner must still record how generated material reaches learners or the public.

## Evidence and exceptions

- Each registered system must have a current owner, use description, exposed group, disclosure status, human-review path, and evidence reference.
- `missing`, `stale`, `conflicting`, and `not-applicable` are different evidence states. Missing evidence must not be converted to compliance or non-compliance.
- An exception requires a named owner, rationale, expiry date, and Legal approval. The automation may draft an exception request but may not approve it.

## Approval boundary

Legal owns final interpretation of the EU AI Act. Operations owns policy activation and operational deadlines. System owners correct evidence. The automation may prepare a draft impact package and proposed actions; it may not give final legal advice, activate policy, change a production deadline, close an incident, or submit an official response.
